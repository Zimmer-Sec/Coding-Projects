## The Sun’s Unstoppable Threat: Coronal Mass Ejection:

Simply put, our Sun is constantly emitting solar winds of electromagnetic radiation, which is shielded by our planet's magnetic field and allows life to exist. When higher levels of electromagnetic activity are captured by Earth's magnetic field, you can see this phenomenon as the Aurora Borealis. The Sun's atmosphere has an outer layer called the Corona. Within the Corona, there are a couple of events that can cause a magnetic buildup of plasma (electrically charged gas) or a stressed twist of magnetic structures. When these pressures are released, they create what's called a Coronal Mass Ejection, which disperses electromagnetic energy as a solar flare or accelerates solar storms ahead of them (for more information, read about Magnetic Reconnection and plasma filaments/prominences). These Coronal Mass Ejections, or any intense geomagnetic or solar storm for that matter, when aimed at Earth, have the ability to severely disrupt our society's interconnected way of life. As we'll discuss in a later section, this kind of unexpected event would undoubtedly resurface our society's animalistic instincts as soon as one is announced.

## A History Lesson: The Carrington Effect:

During the late Summer of 1859, people as far south as the Caribbean Sea watched the night sky light up with the Aurora Borealis. At that time, they didn’t understand the connection between geomagnetic activity, solar storms, and the Aurora phenomenon. Telegraph systems, compasses, and magnetic activity readers were acting strange and some reportedly saw electrical surge damage (Briggs, 2023). A few days later, an astronomer by the name of Richard Carrington witnessed large bursts of bright light from dark spots on the Sun. After around seventeen hours, all of North America was lit up like it was daytime. This bright flash was a massive Coronal Mass Ejection that led to a severe solar storm and what we now call the Carrington Effect.

## Amplified Threats to Modern Society:

If a CME caused serious electromagnetic damage in 1859... one can only begin to imagine the impact it would have on modern society. In severe cases, FEMA states that geomagnetic events can cause widespread power grid voltage problems and blackouts as well as HF and GPS signal disruption. In a worst-case scenario, power grids are overcharged and suffer irreversible damage due to geomagnetically induced currents. Communications systems are largely disrupted or destroyed and essential services are no longer available. Water distribution systems lose power entirely.

At this point, unprepared individuals will be scrambling to take what they can before others do the same. Well-mannered citizens will try to stay calm, but sheer numbers of unprepared people will overwhelm any sense of community order.

This is the reality of the situation. Unlike a pandemic, logistics systems can't function and restocking isn't coming for weeks or months. Federal response, however capable, cannot reach very many people in time. **How would you handle this situation knowing help isn't coming?** That's the question I'm using the power of modern software to help answer.

## CME Tabletop Exercise

Developed through flipped interaction techniques (shout-out Dr. Jules White), please copy/paste the [prompt](broken) into your favorite LLM. It was built in-line with guidance published by NOAA's Space Weather Prediction Center, FEMA Ready.gov, and NERC, and is modeled loosely on professional emergency management tabletop methodology. It will ask you a few questions about your actual life, then put you through a realistic scenario tailored to your household, location, and current preparedness level. At the end, you'll get a structured after-action report and a gap analysis showing specifically what you'd be missing.

It may take up to 30 minutes to complete, but it might actually save your life.
