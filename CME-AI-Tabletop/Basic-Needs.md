# Essential Items to Have On-hand

#### All of this costs under a thousand dollars and can be acquired online over a period of time. Please please PLEASE start picking up the most essential elements of this list and packaging it in your home for safe-keeping. These items become the difference between life and death somewhere around day 3 to day 5 (the window FEMA targets with its 72-hour preparedness baseline and exactly why that baseline exists.) Also, remember to have a method of protecting your family and self during trying times.

----

### Emergency Detection & Alerting
- Smoke detectors (2-pack): $25
- Carbon monoxide detector: $30
- NOAA Weather Radio (hand-crank or battery-powered): $35
- Fire extinguisher (ABC-rated, 5 lb minimum): $45

### Water
- Water filtration pitcher or LifeStraw-style filter: $35
- Water purification tablets: $10
- 7-gallon water storage containers (x2): $30
- 72-hour bottled water minimum (1 gal/person/day): $25

### Food
- 72-hour non-perishable food supply per person: $50
- Manual can opener: $10
- Portable camp stove + fuel canisters: $45

### First Aid & Medical
- Well-stocked first aid kit (Red Cross recommended): $40
- 30-day personal medication supply: $30 (varies)
- Thermometer: $15
- Pain relievers, antacids, anti-diarrheal medications: $20
- N95 respirators (box of 10): $20

### Tools & Utility Shutoff
- Basic tool kit (hammer, pliers, screwdrivers): $40
- Adjustable wrench for gas/water shutoff: $15
- Duct tape: $10
- Heavy-duty work gloves: $15
- Utility shutoff key: $10

### Power & Light
- Flashlights (x2) + alkaline batteries: $25
- Set of Headlamps: $20
- Portable power bank (20,000+ mAh): $35
- Surge protector power strip: $25

### Documents & Information
- Fireproof document box or safe: $50
- USB drive with digital copies of critical documents: $15
- Printed local maps (no GPS dependency): $5

### Sanitation
- Emergency toilet/bucket kit or waste bags: $20
- Hand sanitizer (bulk): $12
- Hygiene kit (travel toothbrush, soap, wipes): $15
